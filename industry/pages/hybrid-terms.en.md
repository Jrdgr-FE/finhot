# Terms of use

English translation of the published Chinese terms, version 1.1, effective 3 October 2026. The Chinese version remains the reference text.

Operator: the FinHot maintainer. Contact: the on-site feedback page, /feedback.

FinHot is an independently maintained financial news index offering summaries, categories, selected stories, daily briefs and RSS. No registration is required to browse. English titles and summaries are translations of the available public copy; they may become available after the original-language entry.

## Content and sources

Titles, summaries and categories may be generated by models using publicly available material. Web extraction can fail, and only some pages or excerpts of a PDF or long document may be processed. The coverage shown on each page describes the material used; it does not mean the complete report has been reviewed.

Models may misread figures, omit conditions or make mistakes. Open the original source to verify important information. Selection does not imply endorsement and is not investment advice. Research opinions, corporate disclosures and reported facts should be checked in their original context.

Copyright in original material belongs to its respective owners. FinHot displays summaries and source links, rather than republishing full articles. The source list may change. A source that is absent, unavailable or no longer updating is not necessarily covered.

## Quoting and use

You may use the site for personal reading and internal institutional research. When quoting summaries prepared by FinHot, credit FinHot and retain the original source link. Third-party material remains subject to its owner's terms. Contact the maintainer through the feedback page before bulk republication or commercial use of FinHot's summaries.

RSS and public APIs provide read-only access. Keep request frequency reasonable and do not disrupt the service.

## Corrections and service

Sources and readers can report errors in content, attribution or links through the feedback page, including the relevant page, an explanation and supporting evidence. The maintainer may correct, adjust or remove content after verification. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or unrelated personal information.

FinHot does not guarantee completeness, accuracy, real-time updates or continuous availability. Collection, connectivity, model budgets and maintenance may affect updates. Entries can appear with a source headline and link before a summary or translation is ready. Public results target a two-minute synchronization interval, in addition to collection and processing time. If synchronization is interrupted, the last complete snapshot remains available. The browsing index covers up to 5,000 recent public entries and the report directory lists up to 30 daily editions; previously published article and report URLs remain archived unless withdrawn.
